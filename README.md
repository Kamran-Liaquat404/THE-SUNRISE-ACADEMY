## 📖 About

**The Sunrise Academy Kharan** is an educational institution dedicated to providing quality schooling, strong conceptual foundations, and focused examination preparation for students in Kharan, Balochistan, Pakistan.

The institution emphasizes a comprehensive school curriculum spanning Early Years, Primary, Middle, and Secondary education, complemented by specialized academic preparation classes in Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Mathematics for 9th & 10th-grade board examination success and medical preparation guidance.

---

## 🎯 Educational Programs & Structure

### School Programs
- **Early Years**: Play & Concept Discovery, Phonics, Numeracy
- **Primary Education**: Literacy Fluency, Core Sciences, Mathematics
- **Middle School**: Analytical Thinking, Pre-Algebra, General Science
- **Secondary Education**: Matric Board Curriculum, Intensive Concept Review

### Academy & Subject Preparation (9th & 10th Class)
- **Physics**: Mechanics, Electricity, Magnetism, Numerical Problem-Solving
- **Chemistry**: Atomic Structure, Periodic Trends, Stoichiometry, Equations
- **Biology**: Human Physiology, Cell Biology, Medical Entrance Guidance
- **Mathematics**: Algebra, Trigonometry, Geometry, Board Test Series
