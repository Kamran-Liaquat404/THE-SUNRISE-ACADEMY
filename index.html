Frequently Asked Questions

Common Queries & Guidance

Answers to frequently asked questions regarding our school curriculum, academy classes, and admission procedures.

The Sunrise Academy operates both as a comprehensive school (providing Early Years, Primary, Middle, and Secondary schooling) and as an academic preparation academy offering focused 9th and 10th class preparation, board exam revision, and pre-medical subject coaching.

Our academy wing specializes in core matriculation science subjects: Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Mathematics, focusing on conceptual clarity, numerical problem solving, diagrams, and board exam answer formatting.

Parents and students can submit an online Admission Enquiry through this website or reach out directly using the Call or WhatsApp buttons.

The institution is located in Kharan, Balochistan, Pakistan, serving scholars and families across Kharan with quality educational facilities and dedicated faculty.
